How it works

Generate captions

Pick a language and template, then time your captions on the timeline in the editor.

Export SRT

Download the .srt file — one entry per caption with exact in/out times.

Import into Premiere / Resolve

Drop the SRT on your timeline; style with your NLE's native caption tools or use the alpha WebM.
