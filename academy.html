Fundamentals

Word-by-word karaoke that holds attention

4 min readBeginner

Why highlighting each word as it's spoken increases watch time, and how to time captions on the Kalakar timeline. Word-by-word karaoke keeps viewers glued because their eyes track the highlight, matching the audio rhythm. On the Kalakar timeline, add a caption, set start/end times, and the "Word by Word" mode auto-highlights each word in sequence.

Templates

Designing viral caption templates

6 min readBeginner

Anatomy of the Hormozi pop, Neon Punch and Boxed Pill styles — outline, highlight color and background pill. Hormozi Pop uses thick black stroke + yellow highlight; Neon Punch adds a glowing drop shadow; Boxed Pill wraps each word in a rounded rectangle. Open the Templates tab in the editor and click any card to apply the full style.

Timing

Caption pacing for desi languages

5 min readIntermediate

Hindi, Tamil and Bengali sentences run longer — split captions across the timeline without losing sync. Keep each caption block to 3–5 words for reels, or 6–8 words for long-form. Drag block edges on the timeline to trim; drag the block itself to shift timing. The Auto Caption tool groups words automatically at 5 words per cue.

Audio

Cleaning street noise without a studio

7 min readIntermediate

How highpass, presence EQ and compression rescue captions shot in traffic, crowds or markets. Toggle "AI Noise Cleanup" in the Audio panel: a 90 Hz highpass removes rumble, a 3 kHz presence boost lifts the voice, and a 4:1 compressor evens levels. Apply it on playback and it carries into your export.

Workflow

Premiere Pro & Resolve round-trip

5 min readAdvanced

Export SRT, import into your NLE, keep styles native — plus alpha channel workflows for overlays. In Kalakar, hit "Download SRT", then drag the file onto your Premiere or Resolve timeline. For styled overlays, enable "Alpha Channel" in the Export panel to render a transparent WebM you can composite over any footage.

Growth

Hooks that stop the scroll

4 min readBeginner

Pattern-interrupt captions in the first 3 seconds: what the 200,000+ creators on Kalakar are using right now. Open with a bold claim ("This changed everything"), a POV framing ("POV: You finally figured it out"), or a curiosity gap ("Wait for it…"). The Templates tab has ready-made hook styles — click one and type your line.
