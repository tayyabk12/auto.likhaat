Delete Account

Permanently remove your account and all associated data

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your profile, name and email are permanently removed.
  • All saved projects in your Library are deleted.
  • Active subscriptions are cancelled immediately.
  • Unused transcription credits are forfeited.
  • Data is purged from our systems within 30 days.

Before you delete

We recommend exporting your projects first. Go to the Editor → Export tab → Export Project JSON to save a backup. You can re-import it anytime if you create a new account.

Danger Zone

This action is irreversible. All your data will be permanently deleted.

Hosted product

If you have an account on the hosted app.kalakar.io, use the in-app Delete Account feature or email support@kalakar.io from your registered address.
