Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Overview

Kalakar is built with privacy at its core. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. What We Collect

Account information: Name and email address when you create an account.

Payment information: Processed securely via Stripe. Kalakar does not store your card details.

Usage data: anonymized analytics to improve the product (pages visited, features used).

3. Video & Audio Content

Hosted product: Videos you upload are processed to generate captions and are stored securely in cloud storage for the duration of your project. They are never shared with third parties.

Open-source build: Videos never leave your browser. All processing (transcription, enhancement, rendering) happens locally on your device. No video or audio data is transmitted to any server.

4. AI Transcription

In the open-source build, transcription runs locally in your browser using Whisper (via Transformers.js). The model file is downloaded once from a public CDN and cached in your browser. Your audio is never sent to a transcription server.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We use privacy-respecting analytics cookies. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Cloud storage providers — to store your projects (hosted product only)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time, which removes all associated data within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Export your projects
  • Object to processing

9. Children's Privacy

Kalakar is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified via email or in-app notice.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@kalakar.io.
