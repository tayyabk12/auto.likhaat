Caption Languages

Auto-generate captions in 20 desi & regional languages — up to 97% accuracy

How it works

Each language uses Whisper (via Transformers.js) running locally in your browser. The first time you transcribe in a language, the model downloads once and caches. After that, transcription works offline.

For best accuracy in Desi languages, use the Small model (~250MB). The Base model (~80MB) is a good balance of speed and accuracy.
